## Henry Heffernan

A huge and heartfelt thank-you to [Henry Heffernan](https://github.com/henryjeff) for the original desktop portfolio concept, shell, 3D workstation direction, and the creative foundation that inspired this project.

The assets, layout ideas, and personality of the original work made this project possible in the form it has today, and I’m genuinely grateful for the time and craft behind them.
