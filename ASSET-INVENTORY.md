## Legal and practical note

Downloaded assets are useful for inspecting how the reference works. Before shipping your own site, replace them with assets you created, commissioned, or licensed. In particular, replace the 3D models, Yaru icons, Spotify playlist, social links, project copy, and any personal photographs or documents.
